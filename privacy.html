Privacy Policy

Clip & Cam: Reaction Recorder

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Overview

Clip & Cam records you with the camera and microphone of your Mac and combines the recording with a video file you choose. It works entirely on your Mac, has no network access, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. The app does not:

• Collect personal information
• Track your usage or behavior
• Upload your recordings or files to any server
• Use analytics or tracking services
• Use cookies or similar technologies
• Share any data with third parties

Camera and Microphone

The camera and microphone are used only while you record a take. Recordings are saved to the Movies/WordClip folder on your Mac and stay there until you delete them.

File Access

The app reads the video files you open and writes its takes and finished videos to Movies/WordClip.

On-Device Processing

All recording, mixing and exporting happens locally. Settings are stored in the app's preferences on your Mac and are not transmitted anywhere.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the changes on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
