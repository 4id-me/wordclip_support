Clip & Cam: Reaction Recorder

Record yourself under a clip: short vertical reaction videos, like explaining a word from a song.

About

Clip & Cam puts a fragment of a music video on top and your camera below. You record yourself once: introduce the word, sing the line along with the clip, then explain what the word means. The app builds a vertical video ready for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Features

  • Split vertical video — the clip above, you below, divided at the golden section; 1080×1920 mp4
  • Pick the fragment — only the part of the clip you choose is played
  • Teleprompter under the camera — one gliding row at the top of the screen, so you look into the lens; it starts the clip by itself at a [clip] line
  • Sing-along words — lines between two [clip] lines are shown while the clip plays
  • Subtitles — made from the teleprompter script, timed as you read
  • Mix after recording — your voice and the clip volume, trimming, clip length, caption and a fading thumbnail title can all be changed without recording again
  • Trim to voice — silence before and after your speech is cut automatically
  • English and Russian interface

How to Use

  • Record tab: Open file… and choose a video with the song; set the start and end of the fragment
  • Paste your script into Teleprompter; put a line [clip] where the clip should play
  • Put on headphones, press Record (⌘R), read the script, sing along with the clip, explain the word, press Stop
  • Mix tab: adjust volumes, trims, caption and title, then Export video
  • The video is saved to Movies/WordClip

FAQ

Why headphones?

The clip is added to the video from the file itself. Without headphones the microphone would record it a second time from the speakers.

Can I use any music video?

Clip & Cam works with video files you already have on your Mac. You are responsible for having the right to use the music and video in what you publish.

Where are my recordings?

In Movies/WordClip: raw takes in takes, finished videos next to them. Nothing leaves your Mac.
